Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Hearthold is a game made by Nanyka Hub ("we"). This page says what the game keeps about you, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. Questions: contact@profitpathagent.com.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. The game makes an anonymous player ID for you.
  • We keep your game progress on our game server so it is not lost.
  • We only collect play statistics if you say yes, and you can change your mind any time in Settings.
  • We never see your card or bank details. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store.
  • We do not sell your data and the game shows no ads.

What we keep

WhatWhyWhen
An anonymous player IDTo know which save is yours.Always, from the first launch.
Your game progress: your town, Keepers, gear, currencies, stages, trophies, raids, friends and settings To keep your save safe and run the game (raids, leagues, friends).Always.
Your street name and Keeper, trophies and town layout Other players see these when they find or raid your street, and on leaderboards.Always. Street names may be typed by you; do not use your real name.
Your email, if you sign in Signing in (by email, Google or Apple, through Unity Player Accounts) lets you keep your save on a new phone and use friends. We receive an account ID from Unity; we do not receive your password.Only if you choose to sign in.
Purchase records: what you bought, the store's order or purchase token To check with the store that a purchase is real before giving you what you bought, and to stop it being given twice. Only when you buy something.
Play statistics: what you do in the game (stages, raids, upgrades, purchases), your device model, operating system and how smoothly the game runs, and which link or advert led you to install the game To find which parts are too hard, where new players stop, and what to fix. Only if you answer YES to "Help make Hearthold better?". Never your name, contacts or anything you type.

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Who else handles it

  • Unity Technologies runs our game server (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Remote Config, Analytics, Player Accounts). Your data is stored on Unity's servers. Unity's privacy policy.
  • Google (Google Play) and Apple (App Store) handle payments and tell our server whether a purchase is real. They also deliver the game's notifications. Google's privacy policy, Apple's privacy policy.

We share your data with nobody else, and we never sell it.

Notifications

If you allow them, the phone reminds you when something in your town has finished (an upgrade, a full Hearth). They are made on your phone; you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

How long we keep it

Your save is kept while you play. If you delete your account, your save, street and friends are deleted from our server, and play statistics linked to your player ID are deleted too. Purchase records may be kept as long as the law requires for accounting.

Your choices and rights

  • Play statistics: turn them on or off any time in Settings. You can also ask us to delete what was sent.
  • Delete your account: in the game, Settings → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT. Or follow these steps without the game.
  • See or correct your data: email us with your player ID (in Settings → ACCOUNT).

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you have the right to see, correct, delete or move your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Hearthold is made for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Big changes will also be told in the game.
