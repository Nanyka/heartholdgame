Delete your Hearthold account

Hearthold, by Nanyka Hub

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Hearthold and tap the gear (top right) to open Settings.
  2. Open the ACCOUNT tab and tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the game

Email contact@profitpathagent.com with the subject "Delete my Hearthold account", and include either:

  • your player ID (Settings → ACCOUNT in the game), or
  • the email you signed in with, if you signed in.

We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your save: town, Keepers, gear, currencies, stages, trophies and settings.
  • Your street, its name and its place on leaderboards; your friends list and friend code.
  • Play statistics linked to your player ID.

What may be kept

  • Records of purchases, for as long as tax and accounting law requires (usually several years).
  • Sparks and anything bought cannot be restored after deletion.
